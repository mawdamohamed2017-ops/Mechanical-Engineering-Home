Maintenance best practices

Criticality first

Rank assets by safety, environmental and production consequence before choosing a strategy.

Plan the work

Scope, parts, tools, permits and safe isolation defined before the job is scheduled.

Fix causes

Run RCFA on repeat and high-consequence failures; track actions to closure.

Measure

Track MTBF, MTTR, PM compliance, backlog weeks and planned-work percentage.

RefineryMech is a general technical reference. It distinguishes general engineering guidance from codes and standards, OEM recommendations and site-specific procedures; where they differ, the latter govern. Always follow lock-out/tag-out, gas testing and permit-to-work rules.
